# Snowball Run

**Start as a snowball. Finish as an avalanche.**

### [Play it now in your browser](https://r4ij3n-droid.github.io/snowball-run/)

You're rolling down a mountain, and every second you're either getting bigger or getting smaller. Drag left and right to pick your lane. Blue gates grow you. Red gates eat away at you. Some rows make you choose between +40 and ×2, and the right call depends on how big you already are.

Anything with a green number is smaller than you, so roll straight through it. A red number means it's bigger, and it'll take a chunk out of you.

Then you hit the bottom of the hill: ten walls, marked ×1 to ×10. Every wall you smash multiplies your coins. Get big enough and you break all ten.

**What's in it**

- One-finger controls. Drag to steer (mouse and arrow keys work too).
- Endless levels across four looks: daytime slopes, sunset, aurora nights and a glacier.
- Upgrades for your starting size, snow pickup and coin bonus, plus six snowball skins.
- Plays in portrait or landscape, in English or Arabic.
- The whole game is under 1 MB, so it loads instantly.

Made for YouTube Playables.
